# Project Feedback Video Script (2–3 minutes)

Hello, my name is Ira Khan. This video explains my Nassau Candy Product Line Profitability and Margin Performance Analysis project.

The objective of the project was to understand which products actually drive profit instead of looking only at sales volume.

I used Python, pandas, NumPy, Plotly and Streamlit. The analysis covers product profitability, gross margin, profit per unit, division performance, cost diagnostics, Pareto concentration and geographic revenue concentration.

First, the dashboard provides KPI cards for revenue, gross profit, cost, gross margin and units.

The Product Profitability tab allows users to compare products by total profit, gross margin, profit per unit and contribution.

The Division Performance tab compares Chocolate, Sugar and Other using revenue, cost, gross profit and margin.

The Cost Diagnostics tab compares cost ratio against gross margin to identify products that deserve closer pricing or sourcing review.

The Pareto tab shows how concentrated profit is across products and also provides a geographic revenue view.

One important data-quality limitation is that the source dataset has unrealistic order and ship-date ranges. I therefore avoid treating lead-time results as real-world delivery performance.

The main learning from this project was that revenue and profitability are not the same thing. A good profitability dashboard needs multiple metrics so that management can distinguish volume, margin efficiency and cost burden.

Thank you for reviewing my project.
